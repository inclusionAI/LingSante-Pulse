# Third-party materials

Third-party datasets and model assets remain subject to their upstream licenses. Obtain those assets from their original providers and follow their licenses and data-use terms. Upstream links and pinned revisions are provided in DATA.md and the download scripts.

The CGMformer model-loading adapter follows the released CGMformer architecture and position embedding. CGM-JEPA/X-CGM-JEPA loading uses the original upstream encoder. The GlucoFM baseline code is a local reconstruction from its paper and is not official Google code.

The distributed pretrained model contains learned parameters and fitted aggregate statistics, not training examples. Metadata removal does not establish a formal privacy guarantee for the learned weights. No medical use or clinical performance claim is made by this software package.
